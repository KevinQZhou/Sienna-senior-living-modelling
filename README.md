# Sienna Senior Living Inc. – Financial Modelling & Valuation

## Project Overview

An independent financial modelling and equity research project focused on Sienna Senior Living Inc. (TSX: SIA), a Canadian owner and operator of seniors' living and long-term care communities.

The objective is to evaluate the company's historical financial performance, operating fundamentals, capital structure, and valuation through an integrated financial model.

## Project Objectives

- Develop an integrated three-statement financial model.
- Analyze historical financial performance and operating trends.
- Forecast revenue, expenses, working capital, and cash flows.
- Build supporting debt and property acquisition schedules.
- Perform Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation.
- Conduct comparable company valuation analysis.
- Evaluate valuation sensitivities and key investment considerations.

## Methodology

**1. Historical Financial Analysis**
- Review annual financial statements and management discussion and analysis.
- Analyze revenue, operating expenses, profitability, and cash flow trends.
- Evaluate key operating metrics, including occupancy and AFFO.

**2. Financial Modelling**
- Build historical financial statements.
- Develop revenue and operating expense forecasts.
- Construct supporting schedules for debt, depreciation, acquisitions, and working capital.
- Integrate the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement.

**3. Valuation**
- Discounted Cash Flow analysis.
- Comparable company analysis.
- Sensitivity analysis across key valuation assumptions.
